About Us

A firm built for the congregations it serves

WW Housing Development LLC exists for one reason: churches and community groups across Houston are sitting on land that could become housing, and almost none of them have been given a straight, plain-language path to building it.

The Firm

Why this firm, and why now

Many churches in the Houston area own land or buildings they no longer fully use. Those organisations hold something a developer cannot buy — deep trust in their own neighbourhood — but little experience with development financing, tax credits, or government programmes. Meanwhile, recently passed federal affordable housing legislation is expected to release new funding.

No consulting firm in Houston currently serves that gap in a clear, accessible way. We were formed to fill it: an attorney and a pastor, working on the same side of the table as the congregation, from the first look at a property through to the day residents move in.

The two people you will work with

T. Deon Warner, Esq.

Co-Founder & Principal

Deon is an attorney and the principal of Warner & Associates PLLC in Houston. His practice covers transactional real estate, corporate governance, tax planning, nonprofit and church property matters, and litigation finance.

He has structured complex church property transactions, seller-financed closings, tax credit deals, and development agreements for Houston-area projects — the parts of a development where congregations are most exposed and least often represented.

Rev. Dr. Marilyn White

Co-Founder & Principal

Rev. Dr. Marilyn White is a community advocate and nonprofit executive with more than three decades of leadership across the corporate, nonprofit, and faith-based sectors. Before answering the call to ministry, she spent twenty-two years as an executive underwriter in commercial insurance, where she built her expertise in business management, strategic planning, risk analysis, and organisational leadership.

Today she serves as Senior Pastor of St. James United Methodist Church in Houston’s historic Third Ward, while continuing to lead initiatives that strengthen communities through partnership and advocacy. She is widely recognised for turning vision into action — securing more than $1 million in charitable dental services for underserved residents, and providing leadership for the development of a $33 million affordable senior housing community.

She is a retired Elder in Full Connection in the United Methodist Church, and her work reflects a philosophy that pairs compassionate service with sound organisational strategy and measurable community impact.
